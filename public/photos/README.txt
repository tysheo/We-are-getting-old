Drop image files here (.jpg .png .webp), then run:

    node tools/local_photos.mjs

and reload the app. Your photos flow through the Act 3 archive tunnel and the
Act 5 collage. Re-run whenever you add or remove images.

This is the no-setup path for inserting your own pictures. The full Python
pipeline (tools/harvest_*.py) is for pulling from museum / stock APIs instead.
